IELTS Writing: criteria, practice and sample answers

The Writing test lasts 60 minutes and has two tasks. Both must be done, and Task 2 carries more weight in the marking than Task 1. Examiners assess each task on four criteria, weighted equally.

These pages take the four criteria one at a time, with weak and better examples of each, so that you know what an examiner is looking for before you write. Then come practice questions of our own for Task 2, three complete sample essays with comments on each criterion, practice for Academic Task 1 with data tables, and letter practice for General Training Task 1. Work through a criterion page, then write a timed answer, then compare it with a sample. Writing improves only by writing and checking.

  • Coherence and cohesion
  • Lexical resource: vocabulary
  • Grammatical range and accuracy
  • Task 2 practice questions
  • Sample essay: opinion
  • Sample essay: discuss both views
  • Sample essay: problem and solution
